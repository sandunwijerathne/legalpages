What this app is

The app is a private tool that the operator uses to publish news and information videos to Facebook Pages that the operator manages. It is not offered to the public. Visitors and followers of those Pages do not sign in to it or use it.

Information the app uses

  • Facebook Page information: the names and IDs of the Pages the operator manages, and an access token for each Page.
  • Published content: the videos, cover images, captions and hashtags that the app uploads, and the ID and link Facebook returns for each post.

The app uses these Facebook permissions: to list the operator's Pages (pages_show_list), to read Page information (pages_read_engagement) and to publish to those Pages (pages_manage_posts).

The app does not collect personal information about people who view, follow, react to or comment on the Pages. It does not read their profiles, friends or messages.

How the information is used

Page IDs and access tokens are used only to publish and schedule the operator's own posts and to check that publishing works. The information is not used for advertising or profiling, and it is not sold or rented.

Where it is stored

Access tokens, Page IDs and records of published posts are stored on the operator's own computer. Working copies of videos and images are deleted after publishing or by a daily clean-up.

Sharing with other services

  • Meta (Facebook): receives the videos, cover images and captions so that they can be published. Meta's own privacy policy applies to what it receives.
  • Image generation services: to make illustrations, the app may send a written description of a picture to an image service such as Pollinations or OpenAI. No Facebook data, access tokens or information about Page followers is sent to them.
  • Text assistance: if the operator turns it on, news text may be sent to Anthropic (Claude) to help write headlines and summaries. No Facebook data or access tokens are sent.

Information is otherwise disclosed only if the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting information

Page IDs and access tokens are kept only while the app is used for publishing. They can be removed at any time. See the data deletion instructions.

Security

Access tokens are kept in a private settings file that is not published online and is blocked from web access. No method of storage is completely secure; if a token may have been exposed, it is revoked and replaced.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect information from them.

Changes and contact
