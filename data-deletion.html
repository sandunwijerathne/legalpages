Data Deletion Instructions

App: France Now (a publishing tool, "Post Creator")
Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL]
Last updated: 27 November 2026

This app is used only by its operator to publish to the operator's own Facebook Pages. It does not collect personal information about people who view or follow those Pages.

Remove the app's access in Facebook

  1. Sign in to Facebook.
  2. Open Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations.
  3. Select France Now and choose Remove.

This ends the app's access and stops its access tokens from working.

Ask for your data to be deleted

If you believe the app holds any information about you, send an email to [CONTACT EMAIL] with the subject "Data deletion request". Include your name, the Facebook Page involved and what you would like deleted. Do not send passwords or access tokens.

We will confirm receipt, and within 30 days we will delete the information we hold about you, including any stored tokens and records, unless the law requires us to keep it. We will tell you when this is done.

Posts on Facebook

Posts that have already been published are stored by Facebook. To have a post removed, ask us at the address above, or use Facebook's reporting tools on the post.
